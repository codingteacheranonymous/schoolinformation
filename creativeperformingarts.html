Blurb:

Is your child bored and needs to be engaged? Do you want your child to have a fun time dancing?
Dance Haven provides a large age group with fun, engaging dance lessons, meeting once a week and
rehearsals close to annual concerts usually in November.
Jess is the name of the organiser and she hosts the concerts, which allows parents to see a
showcase of their children and others performing a show. They have annual movie nights and
end-of-year parties that you can go to and have lots of fun. With a concert theme every year, the dances show
uniqueness and what can happen when community and the art of dance come together. To help express,
build confidence, inspire, boost dancers and relieve the stress from competitive dance, Dance Haven loves growth, love and fun.
Focusing on thriving, Dance haven aims to spark creative minds and start friendships. Always willing to improve dance skills,
Dance haven loves to make concerts lively and entertaining. Paving the path for new dancers, Dance Haven makes every turn memorable and exciting.
